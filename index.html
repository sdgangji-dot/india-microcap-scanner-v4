Tab 5: Free-Float Absorption Squeeze Analyzer — Plain-English Guide

What it means: If a company is worth ₹500 Cr and the owners (Promoters) hold 70%, only 30% (₹150 Cr) is available for the public to trade (Free Float). If an institution buys ₹25 Cr worth of shares in one month, they swallowed up 16.6% of all available public shares!

How a beginner should use it: When Free-Float Absorption % is above 1.5% to 2.0% in a small company, it creates a scarcity of shares ("Float Squeeze") so good earnings news pushes the stock price up rapidly.
